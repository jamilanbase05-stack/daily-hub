How it works

Bank messages: copy a message from your SMS app and press Paste. The app finds the amount, whether it is money in or out, and the balance. News, quotes and Telegram channels refresh automatically when you open the app with internet. Private messages from Facebook and TikTok cannot be read by a normal app, so use the Social inbox for those.
